# Brand icons

Taken from SVG Logos (https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos, via
`@iconify-json/logos`). Logos that are near-black or white were placed on a
white rounded tile so they stay visible on a dark background.

These are trademarks of their respective owners, included only so a Widget can
show which service it relates to. Magibar is not affiliated with or endorsed by
any of them.
